# Shoreditch warehouse-conversion rentals — 30 August 2026 (refresh)

Query: find the best price per advertised bedroom for an active whole-property rental in Shoreditch that is an explicit warehouse conversion.

Method: Rightmove's own Shoreditch residential-results page was retrieved with the keyword `warehouse conversion` and its 25 returned listings were programmatically parsed; two exact keyword matches were found (Phipp Street and Deal Street). The active candidate set was then cross-checked against direct agent/portal pages for core-Shoreditch listings and the closest E1 fringe alternatives. Each listing was included only when its page presented an enquiry/viewing route, except a cheaper Phipp Street listing retained to document that it is **Let agreed**.

Scope: Core Shoreditch means EC2A/Shoreditch Triangle. E1 around Brick Lane, Spitalfields, or Aldgate is labelled `shoreditch_fringe` and cannot displace the core recommendation. "Price per room" means gross monthly advertised rent divided by advertised bedrooms, rounded to the nearest pound; it excludes a reception, office, and guest room. Short lets are shown but not treated as like-for-like long-let value.

Result: Charlotte Road is the lowest-price active, explicit warehouse conversion within core Shoreditch, at **£1,667 per advertised bedroom per month** (£5,000 pcm / 3 bedrooms). Alie Street is numerically cheaper at £1,625 per bedroom but is a short let in the Aldgate/Spitalfields fringe.

Caveat: Availability and tenancy term change rapidly. The listing page was active on the research date, but the agent should confirm it before an application.
